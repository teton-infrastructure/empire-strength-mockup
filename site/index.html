Before you join

Questions.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month to month, with no sign-up fee, no mid-year fee and no cancellation fee.

When is someone at the gym?

The gym is open 24/7 for members. The office is staffed Monday to Friday, 10:00 am to 5:30 pm.

Why do discounts have to be set up in person?

We check a student ID or proof of service once, in the office during office hours. After that it bills like any other membership.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Members' kids can use the Thunderdome, our kids area, at no extra cost.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Idaho Falls locals new to Empire get 3 days free, started in the office during office hours. A day pass is $20 and a week pass is $40.

What happens after a paid-in-full year?

It renews automatically for another 12 months unless you opt out before the renewal date. Paid-in-full plans are non-refundable but transferable.

Is this a CrossFit gym?

No. But we are an official HYROX Training Club.
