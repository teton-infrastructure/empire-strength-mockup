# Pitch: Empire Strength and Fitness concept site

**Live preview:** https://empire-strength-mockup.pages.dev (noindex, concept only)

## The one-line pitch
Your gym is already where Idaho Falls gets strong. Your website should say it in the first two seconds, load instantly on a phone, and tell Google and AI the right phone number.

## What changed and why

**1. Strength-first, and open to everyone.**
The hero now reads "Built for strength. Open to all." over a real photo of your rack row, followed by powerlifting, strongman, Olympic lifting, bodybuilding and HYROX. The six disciplines get their own numbered grid. The tone (sharp type, obsidian black, Empire red) is about putting weight on the bar and becoming your best self, with first-time lifters and competitors equally welcome.

**2. The offer up front, every time.**
"Join · $60/mo" and "Try a day · $20" are the first buttons you see. On phones, a sticky bar keeps **Call · Text · Join $60/mo** under the thumb on every screen.

**3. Proof where people decide.**
The hero carries 24/7 · 16,000 sq ft · **4.9★ on Google (160+ reviews)** · $0 sign-up fee, and the award badges (Idaho's Best Gym 2026, Personal Training 2024 & 2025) sit right under it.

**4. One clear membership story.**
Single, Family and Student/Hero plans side by side, plus trial, passes and paid-in-full in one strip, all using the /join page's numbers. No more three versions of the trial on one page.

**5. Coaching as a selling point.**
"Your goals. Your coach. Your next level." with the three paths (start strong, lift with confidence, chase your next level) and all ten coaches with their specialties.

**6. Built for Google and AI search.**
Clean `ExerciseGym` schema with the **correct phone**, address, 24/7 hours, amenities, awards and prices, plus FAQ schema. One H1 per page. When someone asks ChatGPT or Google "24/7 strength gym in Idaho Falls with strongman", the answer is in plain, structured text.

**7. Fast and safe.**
Plain HTML/CSS with a few lines of JS on Cloudflare's edge. The whole page is a fraction of the current 1.45 MB Wix HTML, with no plugins, no PHP and nothing to patch.

## What to confirm with the owner before launch
- Permission to reuse the facility photos and award badges (all pulled from the current site).
- Which free trial is current (3-day or the 7-day page) and the right office hours.
- Family plan wording (home page "family of 5" vs. /join "2 people + kids").
- HYROX details: 8 classes for non-members, 12 for members (per home page).
- Whether to show review quotes (with permission or live from Google), coach photos, and current events.
- Keep or drop the D&D "Empire Mercenary Company" section. It's left off the concept to keep the strength focus.

## How Empire's site stacks up against other Idaho Falls gyms (checked Nov 19, 2026)

| Gym | What their site does | Where it falls short |
|---|---|---|
| **Club Apple** (theclubapple.com, Squarespace, ~64 scripts) | Big "Idaho's #1 Health Club" claim, free trial / 5-day pass offers, app promo | Pricing hidden behind "starting as low as $17.50/week" and a call-in/form; enrollment fee promos; Reddit threads (r/idahofalls) complain about contracts and cancellation |
| **Gold's Gym Ammon** (goldsgym.com/locations/id/ammon) | Hours, amenities list, member quote | National template; contract terms deferred to "contact your location." FitWhere lists a 12-month agreement at $39.99/mo + $39 annual fee + $3 enrollment |
| **Bodifi** (bodifi.com/join, WordPress, ~52 scripts) | Clear price cards ($44.99–$76.99) | Tiered: sauna and studio perks only in upper tiers or other locations; cardio/tanning/red-light focus, not strength |
| **Planet Fitness Idaho Falls** (planetfitness.com) | $24.99 Black Card, 24/7, welcoming tone | Pin-loaded machines and Smith machines, not a strength floor; generic chain page |
| **Anytime Fitness W Broadway** (anytimefitness.com) | "Try us free," 24/7 | No prices ("stop in… we'll build a customized membership"), ~111 scripts, ~3 s load, generic amenity checklist |
| **Downtown Ambition** (downtownambitionid.com) | Simple $60/mo, no sign-up/cancel fees, capped membership | Application required, 18+, no coaching/strength specialty story |

**Takeaway:** nobody in town pairs transparent all-in pricing with a real strength floor, and the big clubs bury price or contract terms. Empire's real advantages ($60 all-in, $0 sign-up, month to month, saunas/cold tubs included, free kids area, 16,000 sq ft of strength gear, 10 coaching teams, Idaho's Best Gym 2026, 4.9★) are exactly what visitors are trying to dig out of competitor sites. The new layout says them in the first screen.

## Why the new layout converts better
1. **First screen = decision screen:** Idaho's Best Gym 2026 badge, "Built for strength. Open to all.", the core facts (16,000 sq ft, 24/7, $60, no contract, $0 sign-up), a primary "Start your 3-day free trial" CTA plus "Join · $60/mo", and a 4.9★ / 160+ reviews strip.
2. **Proof immediately after:** rating, all three Idaho's Best badges, and real coach credentials (IFBB Pro, USAW Level 2, records set by Grace Jack's athletes, official HYROX club).
3. **Show the iron early:** "What's inside 16,000 sq ft" with real floor photos is the second section, not buried.
4. **Empire vs. the typical big-box gym:** a scannable table framed generically (no named competitor claims).
5. **How joining works in 3 steps**, then **Monthly / Paid-in-full toggle** showing $630 (save $90) and $1,000 family (save $200).
6. Sticky Call / Text / Join bar on phones; ~33 KB HTML vs. 1.45 MB on the Wix home page.

Review quotes: Google reviews appear in search snippets (e.g., Birdeye), but the Birdeye page no longer shows the text and Google Maps can't be opened from the box, so no quotes are on the mockup. If Empire OKs it, 2–3 quotes copied straight from their Google profile can be added.
