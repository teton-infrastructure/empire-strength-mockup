# Pitch: Empire Strength and Fitness concept site

**Live preview:** https://empire-strength-mockup.pages.dev (noindex, concept only)

## The one-line pitch
Your gym is already where Idaho Falls gets strong. Your website should say it in the first two seconds, load instantly on a phone, and tell Google and AI the right phone number.

## What changed and why

**1. Strength-first, and open to everyone.**
The hero now reads "Built for strength. Open to all." over a real photo of your rack row, followed by powerlifting, strongman, Olympic lifting, bodybuilding and HYROX. The six disciplines get their own numbered grid. The tone (sharp type, obsidian black, Empire red) is about putting weight on the bar and becoming your best self, with first-time lifters and competitors equally welcome.

**2. The offer up front, every time.**
"Join · $60/mo" and "Try a day · $20" are the first buttons you see. On phones, a sticky bar keeps **Call · Text · Join $60/mo** under the thumb on every screen.

**3. Proof where people decide.**
The hero carries 24/7 · 16,000 sq ft · **4.9★ on Google (160+ reviews)** · $0 sign-up fee, and the award badges (Idaho's Best Gym 2026, Personal Training 2024 & 2025) sit right under it.

**4. One clear membership story.**
Single, Family and Student/Hero plans side by side, plus trial, passes and paid-in-full in one strip, all using the /join page's numbers. No more three versions of the trial on one page.

**5. Coaching as a selling point.**
"Your goals. Your coach. Your next level." with the three paths (start strong, lift with confidence, chase your next level) and all ten coaches with their specialties.

**6. Built for Google and AI search.**
Clean `ExerciseGym` schema with the **correct phone**, address, 24/7 hours, amenities, awards and prices, plus FAQ schema. One H1 per page. When someone asks ChatGPT or Google "24/7 strength gym in Idaho Falls with strongman", the answer is in plain, structured text.

**7. Fast and safe.**
Plain HTML/CSS with a few lines of JS on Cloudflare's edge. The whole page is a fraction of the current 1.45 MB Wix HTML, with no plugins, no PHP and nothing to patch.

- Whether to show review quotes (with permission or live from Google), coach photos, and current events.
- Keep or drop the D&D "Empire Mercenary Company" section. It's left off the concept to keep the strength focus.
